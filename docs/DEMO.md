# ⚡ 90-Second Judge Demo Script

## Timeline & Narrative Flow

| Time | Stage | Action & Explanation |
|:---:|:--- |:--- |
| **0:00** | **Problem & Bill Upload** | Open `https://minchal-deploy.vercel.app/`. Show TNEB bill upload / manual entry. *"Most Indian households receive a monthly electricity bill with a single rupee total, but no visibility into which appliance cost them money."* |
| **0:15** | **Appliance Selection** | Select Refrigerator, Water Heater, AC. Adjust star ratings or runtimes. *"Users select their household inventory in 10 seconds without needing smart meters or hardware sensors."* |
| **0:35** | **Appliance Breakdown** | Click **Analyze Energy Usage**. Show itemized cost breakdown cards (e.g. AC ₹1,403, Fridge ₹450). *"Our deterministic calculation engine normalizes every appliance to the exact bill total."* |
| **0:50** | **Biggest Surprise & Efficiency Gap** | Show the **Biggest Surprise Card** and Energy Score. *"MINCHAL highlights hidden cost drivers like dirty AC filters and degraded seals."* |
| **1:05** | **Action Plan & Budget Optimization** | Navigate to **What can I afford to fix?**. Click `₹1,000` / `₹5,000` preset pills. *"Our ROI optimizer picks actions that maximize rupee savings per rupee spent."* |
| **1:20** | **Bilingual Tamil & Explainability** | Toggle language to **தமிழ்**. Expand **Working Math Steps**. *"Complete transparency: every number is traceable, non-hallucinatory, and accessible in Tamil."* |
| **1:35** | **Closing & Impact** | Highlight CO2 reduction and Solar Payback. *"MINCHAL empowers 300 million Indian households to reduce electricity bills and cut emissions."* |
